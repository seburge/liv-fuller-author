A middle-grade Christmas adventure

The Blessing Box: Slog Dog’s Christmas Crusade

One Christmas wish. One mysterious gift. A year that changes everything.

All Hugh wants for Christmas is a dog. But Santa delivers the Blessing Box instead—a gift that seems empty until Hugh discovers the opportunity it holds.

Determined to show his parents he is ready for a dog, the shy sixth grader sets to work. Along the way, he faces bullies, betrayal, and the challenge of standing up for himself. His mischievous best friend is ready to help, even if it means landing in the doghouse.

Then an accident forces Hugh to make a choice that could cost him his dream. Can he find the courage to do what matters most?

A heartfelt middle-grade Christmas story about an autistic boy, the dog he longs for, and the unexpected ways kindness can change a life.
