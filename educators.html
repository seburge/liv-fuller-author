For teachers, librarians, and reading groups

Stories that start conversations

The Blessing Box is written for late-elementary and middle-grade readers. Its themes include friendship, kindness, responsibility, the courage to be yourself, and giving from the heart.

These conversation starters draw on the book description. They are an introductory resource, rather than a chapter-by-chapter guide.

Download discussion starter

Before reading

  • What makes a gift meaningful? Can an opportunity be a gift?
  • Hugh wants to show he is ready to care for a dog. What responsibilities would that involve?
  • What does it mean to be your true self when others expect something different?

As you read

  • Notice a moment when Hugh shows persistence. What helps him keep going?
  • How do friends help one another? When can helping become complicated?
  • Look for a choice between what a character wants and what someone else needs.

Make kindness tangible

Create an opportunity box: write down kind actions your class could take, then choose one together. Students can write, draw, or share ideas aloud.
