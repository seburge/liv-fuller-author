For parents (grandparents, godparents, aunts, uncles, and special friends...)

Some of the most precious gifts we give our children aren’t wrapped beneath the Christmas tree. They’re the moments we spend together.

This holiday season, snuggle up with your favorite young reader and step into the magical world of The Blessing Box, where an unforgettable Christmas adventure awaits.

Along the way, you’ll share laughter, surprises, a little suspense, and perhaps a few heartfelt conversations about kindness, courage, and the extraordinary power of believing in yourself.

Whether you take an Advent calendar approach and read one of the 20 chapters each night or lose yourselves in the story on a cozy winter afternoon, The Blessing Box offers something increasingly precious in our busy world—time together. It's the best gift busy parents can give themselves.

And who knows? Perhaps a little of the magic you discover within these pages will find its way into your own home. After all, the most meaningful Christmas messages stay with us year round.
