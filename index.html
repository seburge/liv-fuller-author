Meet the Book

The Blessing Box

All Hugh wants for Christmas is a dog. Santa has something else in mind: a mysterious gift that could change everything.

A Christian, whimsical Christmas adventure for late-elementary and middle-grade readers about friendship, courage, and gifts from the heart.

For parents (and other adults)

Give yourself the gift of spending precious time with the special children in your life this holiday season. Read The Blessing Box with them. Each of the twenty chapters reads in less than fifteen minutes. If you start after Thanksgiving and no later than December 1, your shared Christmas adventure will end in time for Christmas, but the message—and perhaps the concept—will stay in your hearts and home year round.
