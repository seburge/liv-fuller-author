Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Blessing Box about?

The Blessing Box follows Hugh, a sixth grader on the autism spectrum who wants a dog for Christmas. Santa’s unusual gift sets him on a yearlong adventure of friendship, kindness, responsibility, and discovering the gifts we give from the heart. Read more about the book.

What genre is The Blessing Box?

Heartwarming, inspirational, Christian-based Christmas fantasy.

What age range is appropriate for The Blessing Box?

This story is perfect for young readers between ages 8 to 12, for the young at heart, and for those younger than 8 as a family read-along.

Who is the book for?

The book is written for late-elementary and middle-grade readers. Parents, grandparents, and other adults can also enjoy sharing the story with a young reader.

Does the story have a Christian perspective?

Yes. The Blessing Box is a Christian Christmas adventure that explores kindness, courage, and giving, with reminders that Jesus is the reason for the season.

Can we read it together during the holidays?

Yes. There are twenty chapters, so you can read one each night or settle in for a longer reading session. The homepage has an invitation for parents and other adults.

Can I read a sample?

You can read Chapter Two, “A Stinky Afternoon”, on this website.

When is The Blessing Box expected to be released?

November 1, 2026.

How will I know when the book is available?

Request a release notification to receive an email when The Blessing Box is available. The signup is for that notification, rather than an ongoing newsletter. Please have an adult complete the form.

What inspired the story?

Hugh and many of the story’s events were inspired by the experiences of Liv’s son. Read Liv’s story to learn more about the inspiration behind the book.

Are there illustrations?

Yes, The Blessing Box has at least one illustration per chapter. All illustrations by Quincy Battieste.

Who created the illustrations?

The illustrations are by Quincy Battieste.

Are there resources for teachers, librarians, and reading groups?

Yes. The educator resources page includes discussion prompts and activities, plus a downloadable conversation starter based on the book description.

How can I ask about an interview, reading, or school visit?

Use the contact page to share your inquiry. For a school or library visit, include your organization, preferred dates, reader age group, and whether you have an in-person or virtual format in mind. The Media page includes a short biography and book information.

Can I provide a review?

Yes! Visit the Share your thoughts form on the Books page. Enter your first name or pen name and your review or comments, then select “Send feedback.” You can also include an email address if you would like Liv to be able to respond. If you’re happy for Liv to quote your review on this website, check the optional permission box. Reviews are sent privately to Liv for review and do not appear automatically on the website. Please have an adult submit the form; parents and guardians can submit on behalf of younger readers.

How is my information used?

Release-notification details are collected to let you know when the book is available. Contact-form details are used to respond to your inquiry. Read Your information for more details.
