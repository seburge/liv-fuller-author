The Blessing Box Experience

Includes a rigid cardboard Blessing Box, three sheets of silver paper, one sapphire-blue envelope, and one metallic-silver envelope seal. $25 (plus tax and shipping).

In the story, Santa gives Hugh a Blessing Box and a letter challenging him to earn and save $500 in the coming year. If Hugh places the $500 in the Blessing Box under the tree on the following Christmas Eve, then Santa will match the $500.

While a child can ask Santa for a Blessing Box, any adult can give a Blessing Box to the special young person in their life, customizing the challenge and reward for the child. With a little creativity, the gift of a Blessing Box could be a wonderful Christmas keepsake with inherent meaning and mentoring from parents, grandparents, godparents, aunts and uncles, and other special friends in a child's life. It takes a village.
