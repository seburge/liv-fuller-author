LIV FULLER — MEDIA KIT

SHORT BIOGRAPHY
Liv Fuller writes inspirational middle-grade fiction about quiet voices, kindness, and the courage to be yourself. She grew up in rural Wisconsin and earned journalism and law degrees from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. Metro Atlanta is her second home. Her work as an attorney and banker supports a life filled with family, rescued pit bulls, and storytelling. The Blessing Box was inspired by her son’s experiences and celebrates people who follow their own rhythm.

BOOK
The Blessing Box
Author: Liv Fuller
Illustration: Quincy Batts
Audience: Late-elementary and middle-grade readers

All Hugh wants for Christmas is a dog, but Santa's unusual gift sends him on a yearlong journey of friendship, responsibility, and unexpected opportunities. A Christian, whimsical Christmas adventure about the courage to be yourself and gifts from the heart.
