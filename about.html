Meet Liv Fuller

I write heartfelt middle-grade stories for readers who see the world a little differently—and for everyone learning to listen.

I grew up in a small town in rural Wisconsin, where childhood meant towering snowdrifts, summer water-balloon battles with my younger siblings, and friendships that have lasted a lifetime.

I earned journalism and law degrees at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, and I’ll always have the heart of a Badger. Metro Atlanta became my second home, where my work as an attorney and banker paid the bills while the storyteller in me kept asking for a turn.

My family fills my heart: my husband, our children and bonus children, and our rescued pit bulls.

The Blessing Box: Slog Dog’s Christmas Crusade grew from experiences in my own family. It is a love letter to those who follow their own rhythm, keep going through hard things, and discover strength they didn’t know they had.

Telling their stories is my passion and privilege.

Warmly,
Liv
