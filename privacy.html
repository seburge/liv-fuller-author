Your information

When you submit a form on this site, the information you enter is stored through Netlify Forms and is available to the site owner.

Book-release notifications

Your name, email address, and request are collected so Liv can notify you when The Blessing Box is available. This signup is for that release notification, rather than an ongoing newsletter.

Contact messages

Your name, email address, selected topic, and message are collected to respond to your inquiry. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Reader feedback

Your first name or pen name, review, optional email address, and permission choice are collected so Liv can read your feedback and respond if needed. Feedback is reviewed privately before any publication. With your permission, Liv may quote your review on this website using the name you provide. Email addresses are not published, and submitting feedback does not sign you up for release notifications. To request removal of your feedback or a published quote, email Liv at the address below.

For adult visitors

Parents, guardians, educators, and other adult readers should complete these forms. Children should ask an adult to get in touch on their behalf.

Questions or removal requests

To ask about your information or request removal from the release-notification list, email livfullerauthor@gmail.com.
